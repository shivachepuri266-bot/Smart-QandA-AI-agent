CAMPUS CENTRAL LIBRARY - STUDENT INFORMATION AND SERVICES GUIDE

1. Building Hours of Operation
- Monday to Thursday: 7:30 AM - Midnight
- Friday: 7:30 AM - 9:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Final Exam Periods: Open 24 Hours continuously starting two weeks prior to exams.
- Holiday Closures: The library is closed on Thanksgiving Day, New Year's Day, and University Winter Recess.

2. Circulation and Borrowing Policies
Library cards are integrated directly into student identification badges.
- Undergraduate Students: May borrow up to 25 items concurrently for a 21-day loan period. Items may be renewed twice online if no hold has been placed by another patron.
- Graduate Students & Faculty: May borrow up to 50 items concurrently for a full academic semester (120 days).
- Course Reserves: High-demand textbooks placed on reserve by faculty may be checked out for 2-hour or 4-hour in-library use only.
- Overdue Fines: Standard collection books incur a late fee of $0.50 per day per item. Reserve materials incur $2.00 per hour overdue to ensure rapid rotation among students.

3. Study Spaces and Noise Zones
- First Floor (Commons): Collaborative zone. Group discussions, talking, and beverage consumption from covered containers are permitted.
- Second Floor: Quiet study zone. Low-whisper conversation only. Cell phone ringers must be muted.
- Third Floor: Silent study zone. Zero talking permitted. Violators will be asked to relocate to lower levels.
- Group Study Rooms: 14 sound-isolated study rooms equipped with 65-inch 4K monitors, whiteboards, and HDMI connectivity. Rooms must be booked at least 2 hours in advance via the online library portal (maximum 3 hours per reservation).

4. Digital Resources and Interlibrary Loan (ILL)
Students have free access to over 300 academic databases, including IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, JSTOR, ScienceDirect, and SpringerLink. For articles or books not held in the university catalog, students can submit an Interlibrary Loan (ILL) request. Digital articles are typically delivered via PDF within 24 to 48 hours. Physical books from partner institutions arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

5. Research Support and Citation Help
Subject specialist librarians are available for one-on-one research consultations on Level 1 (Ask-A-Librarian Desk) or via live chat. Librarians assist with literature search strategies, boolean query structuring, and citation management using Zotero, Mendeley, and EndNote.
